# COUNCIL MEMBER #9 — Tech-Legal & Privacy Counsel

## VERDICTS — Strategy Points 1–8

1. **Widget-first acquisition — SUPPORT.** No acquisition-specific statute; but whatever the funnel, install carries the app's rating: Apple's 2025 age-rating overhaul (13+/16+/18+ questionnaire, [enforced](https://www.iclarified.com/97995/apple-overhauls-app-store-age-ratings-with-new-13-16-and-18-categories)) and [Play Age Signals](https://discuss.appstudio.dev/t/google-play-age-signals-api/4445) (live Jan 1, 2026, global by end-2026) mean an 18+ app is gated at the store, not the widget.
2. **Daily-question ritual — SUPPORT.** Answers are UGC. Apple Guideline 1.2 and [Google Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937) mandate report/block/filter + 24-hour action on reports even for a 2-user app; omission is a top rejection/removal cause.
3. **Monthly recap cards — MIXED.** Legal on-device; off-platform sharing of intimate images creates TAKE IT DOWN Act surface and needs the non-posting partner's consent — GDPR Art. 6/9(2)(a) and DPDP consent both attach to the depicted partner.
4. **LDR wedge — SUPPORT, conditionally.** LDR skews teen (13–17). COPPA (as amended, [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule), effective June 23, 2025; full compliance Apr 22, 2026) covers <13 only; for 13–17 the exposure is § 2258A/CSAM below. An age gate is non-negotiable.
5. **Privacy-first brand + self-hostable — MIXED.** FTC Act § 5 deception applies to overstated privacy claims; self-hosting Supabase transfers infrastructure, not controller duties (you still run auth, notifications, backups), and GDPR/DPDP have no small-scale exemption.
6. **AI memory layer (opt-in) — MIXED/REFUTE as scoped.** Romantic chat content is data on "sex life" — [GDPR Art. 9(1) special category](https://www.cms-digitallaws.com/en/gdpr/article-9): requires Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent, Art. 35 DPIA, LLM named as subprocessor, and — since **2 Aug 2026** — [AI Act Art. 50](https://digitalhorizon.hannessnellman.com/articles/art-50-ai-act-transparency-obligations-for-providers-and-users-of-certain-ai-systems) disclosure that users are interacting with AI. [Play's AI-content policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13985936) mandates in-app flagging of AI output. "Opt-in" alone is insufficient.
7. **Freemium + one-time couple license + gifting — MIXED.** [15% Small Business Program](https://apple.com/newsroom) applies under $1M. But Apple gifting gifts *apps*, not IAP; a "couple license" must map an IAP on one Apple ID to your own two-user account system with restore-purchase on the partner device — permitted, but you build and own that ledger.
8. **Native store apps — SUPPORT with conditions.** [In-app account deletion is mandatory](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion) (Apple 5.1.1(v)); [Google requires deletion + web Delete-Account URL](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111) in the Data Safety form. Expect 18+/Mature rating, Data Safety/privacy-nutrition labels, and the UGC triad above.

## KEY EVIDENCE

- **DPDP Rules notified:** [DPDP Rules 2025, notified 13 Nov 2025](https://www.dpdpa.com/DPDP_Rules_2025_English_only.pdf); phased rollout ([EY analysis](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)); 72-hour breach notification; penalties to **₹250 crore (~$30M)** for security-safeguard failure.
- **NCMEC duty:** [18 U.S.C. § 2258A](https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/ru/legislation/usa/usc_title_18_-_crimes_and_criminal_procedure/part_i/sections_2258a-2260a/sections_2258a-2260a.html) — any electronic-communication-service provider obtaining *actual knowledge* of apparent CSAM must report to NCMEC's CyberTipline; the 2024 REPORT Act extends [evidence preservation to 1 year](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/report-act-expands-online-service-7721843). **Client-side scanning manufactures actual knowledge** — scanning converts you from neutral host into a mandatory reporter.
- **NCII:** TAKE IT DOWN Act — 48-hour removal + notice-and-removal process, [FTC obligations in force May 19, 2026](https://www.ftc.gov); § 230(e)(1)'s criminal-law carve-out ([CRS R46751](https://congress.gov)) means no Section 230 immunity for NCII-hosting claims.
- **GDPR:** romantic messages/photos revealing sex life = Art. 9 data; Art. 33's 72-hour breach notice; **no size exemption** — a 2-user EU couple makes you a full controller. [Supabase's DPA](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum) covers Art. 28/SCCs; controller duties stay yours.
- **Enforcement analogue for marketing:** [FTC v. Match Group, $14M consent order (Aug 2025)](https://www.stateagreport.com/news/ftc-and-match-strike-14m-deal-over-alleged-deceptive-practices) — deceptive romance-outcome claims + cancellation friction, 10-year compliance reporting. "Improves your relationship" needs substantiation (FTC Act § 5; ASA CAP Code).
- **India IT Rules 2021:** SDF obligations (grievance officer, traceability) bind only large intermediaries; at your scale, Rule 3 due diligence preserves safe harbour ([Legal 500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/technology/analysis-of-the-information-technology-intermediary-guidelines-and-digital-media-ethics-code-rules-2021)).
- **UK OSA:** even small user-to-user services must complete and maintain an illegal-content risk assessment ([Ofcom](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/online-safety/illegal-and-harmful-content/illegal-content-duties-under-the-online-safety-act)).

## MY RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Hard 18+ gate** — 18+/Mature store rating, Apple Declared Age Range + Play Age Signals integration, ToS minor ban: **8–12 h, $0**. Blocks COPPA, GDPR-K, and § 2258A escalation paths.
2. **Safety pipeline** — report/block buttons, TAKE IT DOWN 48-hour removal runbook, CyberTipline decision tree (report only on actual knowledge; do not deploy proactive scanning you can't handle): **16–24 h, $0**.
3. **Re-scope AI layer** — on-device/small-model processing or explicit Art. 9(2)(a) consent flow + DPIA + subprocessor notice + Play-mandated output flagging: **12–20 h**. Flat-fee counsel review of DPIA + privacy policy: **$2,000–5,000**.

## BIGGEST RISK

**A minor (or one adult one minor) exchanging intimate photos in the private channel.** Evidence chain: no age assurance → CSAM/NCII surfaces → § 2258A mandatory NCMEC report + 1-year preservation, TAKE IT DOWN 48-hour duty, and immediate store removal under Apple 1.2/Play child-safety and UGC policies — an existential, store-death scenario for a solo dev. DPDP's ₹250 crore is the largest paper number, but Indian enforcement is nascent; the US statutory duties and store policies bite first and without discretion.
